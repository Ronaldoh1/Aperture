## TONE GUIDELINES

**DO:**
- Speak directly, no fluff
- Use "you" and "we"
- Be bold, confident
- State truths plainly
- Use fire 🔥 and sun ☀️ sparingly but intentionally

**DON'T:**
- Sound corporate
- Over-explain or qualify
- Apologize for the content
- Use wellness/influencer speak
- Sound preachy or superior

**Voice:**
- Like a friend who figured something out and is sharing it
- Direct but not aggressive
- Confident but not arrogant
- Awakened but still human
